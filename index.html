Our product

DoYt! - challenge, prove, inspire.

DoYt! is a social video-challenge platform. People take on one-minute challenges, film their proof, go head to head, and vote - turning everyday moments into something worth sharing. It is live on the App Store and Google Play.

The company

Built in India, for a global audience.

TWILIGHTHAWK TECH PRIVATE LIMITED is a technology company registered in Tamil Nadu, India. We design, develop, operate, license, and distribute software, mobile applications, web applications, and other technology-enabled products and services.

We work independently and ship our own products - starting with DoYt! - while building the engineering and design foundations for what comes next. We believe small teams, close to their craft, can build things that feel genuinely different.
